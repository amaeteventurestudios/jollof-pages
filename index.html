The Problem

Keep your story connected
from start to finish

Writing a graphic novel can get messy fast.

Character notes may be in one place. Scripts may be somewhere else. Page ideas may be in another place.

Then continuity starts breaking.

Jollof Pages brings everything together so you can build your story with more control.

Why Jollof Pages Exists

Your story lives in too many places

Most creators use different tools for different parts of the job. That makes it easy to lose track of what is happening. Jollof Pages keeps everything connected.

  • Story notes in one place
  • Scripts somewhere else
  • Character sheets somewhere else
  • Page layouts somewhere else

Capabilities

What Jollof Pages helps you do

Every tool you need to take a story from idea to final page — in one connected environment.

👤

Build your characters

Create rich character profiles with states, history, and continuity tracking. Know where every character stands at every point in your story.

🌍

Shape your world

Define locations, lore, and world rules. Keep your world consistent across chapters, arcs, and volumes.

🎬

Plan scenes, pages & panels

Structure your story from scene level down to individual panels. Map intent and pacing before you draw.

🔗

Track continuity

Catch continuity breaks before they make it to the page. Know when something doesn't line up.

📂

Manage revisions

Keep a clean history of changes. Understand what changed, when, and why — without losing old versions.

🖼️

Organize visual storytelling

Attach visual references to scenes, characters, and panels. Keep art and script aligned throughout production.
