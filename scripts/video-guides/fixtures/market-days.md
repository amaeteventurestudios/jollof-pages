# Before the market opens

The first traders arrive before the sun, carrying **baskets of pepper** and stories older than the town.

# The fish seller

Mama Ekaette has sold smoked fish here for *thirty years*. She knows every customer by the way they bargain.

- Catfish on Mondays
- Bonga on Thursdays
